# NVB earlier analytic ripple comparison (supplement)

![NVB earlier analytic ripple comparison (supplement)](Fig3_ripple_vs_P.png)

**Experiment setup:** From the historical baseline, compute arm-mean Vc peak-to-peak ripple per P step and compare with energy-balance theory.

**Test procedure / measurements:** Check the analytical and measured trends, capacitor capacitance, RMS grid voltage and power/current saturation.

**Recorded result:** An earlier ripple-versus-P reference. It can differ at the 2500 W level due to the old `I_max=15 A` limitation.

**Interpretation:** These statements describe the supplied simulation and its recorded analysis. No new MATLAB or physical HIL/TWIST run was performed to produce this page. Voltage spread, arm-average ripple and low-order sampled-current THD should not be treated as interchangeable metrics.
