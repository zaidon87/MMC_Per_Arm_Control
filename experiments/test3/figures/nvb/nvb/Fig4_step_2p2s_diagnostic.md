# Historical 2.2 s down-step diagnostic

![Historical 2.2 s down-step diagnostic](Fig4_step_2p2s_diagnostic.png)

**Experiment setup:** On the older baseline, zoom around the −1000 W power-reference drop at 2.2 s and inspect the two arm mean voltages.

**Test procedure / measurements:** Look for delayed DC-energy compensation and lower-arm overvoltage; compare with the corrected staircase's bounded slew.

**Recorded result:** Prior model reached about 111.65 V in the lower arm in the older reference. This is **not** a measurement from the corrected T3.3 run.

**Interpretation:** These statements describe the supplied simulation and its recorded analysis. No new MATLAB or physical HIL/TWIST run was performed to produce this page. Voltage spread, arm-average ripple and low-order sampled-current THD should not be treated as interchangeable metrics.
