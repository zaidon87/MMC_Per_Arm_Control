**Experiment setup:** Use the previously archived `grid_base.mat` baseline with enabled local balancing, not the corrected T3.1–T3.3 array.

**Test procedure / measurements:** Check nominal power, current and arm-average Vc, and compare instrumentation or initial parameters only after verifying the different historical run.

**Recorded result:** Historical validation context; do not count as an additional recorded case in the 11-case matrix.

**Interpretation:** These statements describe the supplied simulation and its recorded analysis. No new MATLAB or physical HIL/TWIST run was performed to produce this page. Voltage spread, arm-average ripple and low-order sampled-current THD should not be treated as interchangeable metrics.
